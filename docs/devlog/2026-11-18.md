# 2026-11-18 Devlog

## Task 6 (phase 2): WarpFactory cross-check (notebook Section 7)

### Summary
WarpFactory, the published warp-drive toolkit (Helmerich et al. 2024, CQG 41 095009), was given only the metrics. It independently reproduced the notebook's results: the paper's own Alcubierre example, every total-energy figure, and every Eulerian stress-energy component, including the switching dual shell at 1 GHz. Agreement is at round-off (~1e-15), except the grid-based paper check (7e-5, from the 5 m grid). Its energy-condition maps show the Vorwaller Metric breaking all four conditions throughout the wall and nowhere else.

### Choosing a port
- **NerdsWithAttitudes/WarpFactory:** the MATLAB original (Helmerich, Fuchs et al.), active.

### What Was Done
- `warp/warpfactory_check.py`: WarpFactory receives only a metric function, ds² = −dt² + (dx − S dt)² + …, i.e. shift βˣ = −S.
  - The cosine wall and the dual shell are written afresh as custom metrics.
    - The cosine wall's piecewise definition is wrapped so WarpFactory's own hyper-dual `cos` supplies the in-wall derivatives.
    - The dual shell's centre x_s(t) uses the exact drive antiderivative.
  - The Alcubierre metric is WarpFactory's own.
  - Nothing from warp.curvature or warp.energy is used on the WarpFactory side.
  - Totals use Gauss–Legendre in (r, θ) with the axisymmetric volume element.
- **Results:**
  - **Paper Alcubierre case** (Sec. 4.1: v = 0.1c, R = 300 m, σ = 0.015/m), via WarpFactory's 4th-order finite-difference GridSolver on a 5 m grid:
    - Peak Eulerian energy density: WarpFactory −6.7748e35 J/m³, ours −6.7751e35, paper "≈ −6.8e35".
    - Largest difference anywhere on the grid: 7e-5 of the peak.
  - **Cosine-wall totals** (Δ = 1 m … 1000 m, R = 100 m, β = 1): match the closed form to 3e-15.
  - **Alcubierre with a 1 m wall:** 2e-13.
  - **Full Eulerian T_âb̂ vs `curvature.einstein_frame`:**
    - Moving cosine wall: 1e-15.
    - All four dual-shell variants at 1 GHz (`time_dependent.shift_derivatives`): ≤ 1e-15. This is the first fully independent confirmation of the Section 6 switching terms.
    - The switching terms are ~all of max|T| there, so the check is not dominated by the frozen part.
  - **Energy conditions** (WarpFactory's `get_energy_conditions`, 100 directions × 10 speeds), Δ = 188 m wall:
    - Null and strong are broken at 99.7% of wall points; weak and dominant at 100%. None outside.
    - The 0.3% are where the violation goes to zero: the wall edges and near the axis.
    - The worst sampled observer sees −4.2e38 J/m³, vs −8.4e37 J/m³ for the Eulerian observer (boosted observers see more).
